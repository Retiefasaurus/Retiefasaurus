I am a PhD researcher at the University of Twente, working on physics-based simulations of granular flows. I've worked with, and developed several industrial relevant physics-based solvers, both continuum and discrete. I'm passionate about building cool things.

My interestes are:
* **Everything particles!** (e.g., sugar, sand, skittles)
* **Solid-fluid transition of particles**
* **Constitutive laws** particulary particle-based ones, e.g., rheology, soil mechanics 
* **Physics-based solvers for particles**
* **Particle-based collision detection** e.g., BVH

In my repositories, you'll likely find code related to:
* **Continuum-based solvers** (potentially MPM or related projects)
* **Discrete Element Method** utility scripts
* **CUDA & JAX-based** projects
