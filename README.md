I am a PhD researcher at the University of Twente, working on physics-based simulations of granular flows. 

I've worked with and developed several industrially relevant physics-based solvers, both continuum and discrete.

My interests are:
* **Solid-fluid transition of particles**
* **Constitutive laws** e.g., rheology, soil mechanics 
* **Physics-based solvers for particles**
* **Particle-based collision detection** e.g., Bounding Volume Hierarchy

In my repositories, you'll likely find code related to:
* **Continuum-based solvers** (potentially MPM or related projects)
* **Discrete Element Method** utility scripts
* **CUDA & JAX-based** projects

I’m currently working on ...
* Unifying solid-like and fluid-like constitutive laws
* HydraxMPM, JAX-based benchmarking tool for local and non-homogeneous constitutive laws
